# Regime signals on Monad: rough thresholds

These are starting points, not rules. Always state the figure and its source alongside the judgment.

- MON annualized 24-hour realized volatility: under about 100% is calm for MON, 100% to 200% is ordinary, above about 200% is a spike (measured over late September and early October 2026: median about 114%).
- Monad TVL 7-day change: a move of more than about 10% in token terms is material.
- DEX volume: a day more than twice the 7-day average is a spike; check whether one venue drove it.
- USDC: any reading below 0.99 or above 1.01 USD is material on its own.
- Oracle against pool on the trading venue: more than about 50 bps apart is unusual; the Executor refuses trades past its own deviation limit.
